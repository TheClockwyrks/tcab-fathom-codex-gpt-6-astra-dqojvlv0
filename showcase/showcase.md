# Fathom

A little light. A long way down. Slip into a flooded maze as a tiny glowing forager, gathering plankton from corridors that disappear into the abyss. Every mouthful makes your world a little wider—and your glow a little easier to hunt.

In the Kindle dive, the trench remembers where you have been, but you see it only through a circle of light and memory. Send a sonar pulse down a passage to find the next turn. Watch it split at junctions and curl around rock. Beyond your window, the dark closes in again.

Three hunters listen to the water in different ways. A Lanternjaw follows your light, wearing the same amber disguise as a harmless drifter. An eyeless Gloamfin hears your pulse and answers with its own. A Flarefish turns a patch of darkness into a sudden, dangerous bloom. Drop ink to break a gaze, take a tight corner to shake a chase, and never trust a distant glimmer.

Eat every last mote to clear the maze and descend. Your discoveries and cleared corridors survive a lost life, but deeper water brings more hunters and shorter sonar reach. Keep swimming. The next passage might be your way out.
